ONE SHOT LOGIC — Support

Stuck on something that isn't a puzzle? We're here to help.

Email us: memors.ae@gmail.com

Please tell us your device (for example iPhone 15 or Samsung Galaxy S24), the level number, and what happened. We usually reply within 2 working days.

My purchases are missing

Open the Store in the game and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Level packs, Remove Ads and VIP come back. Power-ups (Hints, Eyes and others) are used up when spent and cannot be restored, the same as in every game.

VIP and the free trial

  • The 3-day free trial is for new VIP members, one per Apple ID or Google account.
  • After the trial, VIP renews monthly unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • To cancel: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, open Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps VIP until the end of the period you have.
  • Everything you have unlocked or won stays yours when VIP ends.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, and only they can issue refunds. To ask for one: Apple — reportaproblem.apple.com; Google Play — Google Play refund help. A refunded purchase is removed from the game.

My progress

Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game, or clearing its data, deletes your progress, so please keep the game installed when you change settings or update it.

Ads and privacy

Ads appear only between levels, never in the middle of one. Remove Ads or VIP turns off automatic ads (videos you choose to watch for rewards stay available). Where it applies, you can change your ad choices in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS. Read our Privacy Policy.

Age

ONE SHOT LOGIC is made for players aged 13 and over.
