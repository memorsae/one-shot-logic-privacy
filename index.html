ONE SHOT LOGIC — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ONE SHOT LOGIC ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress, settings and power-ups are saved on your device only.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking of our own, and we do not sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, ratings, achievements, power-up counts, settings (music, sound, haptics) and similar game data are kept in the game's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought Remove Ads or VIP, the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), your IP address, general device information and how you interact with ads.

  • iPhone / iPad: the game asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before your advertising identifier can be used for personalised ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: where required, a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) lets you choose; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • Optional rewarded videos (for example, a video for a Hint) are only ever shown when you choose to watch them.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases and the VIP subscription are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information; it does not receive your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy. You manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

4. What we do not do

We do not run our own analytics, we do not create user profiles, we do not sell or rent personal information, and the game has no chat, social features or user-generated content.

5. Children

ONE SHOT LOGIC is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It contains mild action and peril (for example weapons, sword fights and escapes) without blood or gore. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; the game itself collects none from anyone (see above). If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared personal information with an advertising or purchase partner, contact us and we will help with its removal.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are best made to the services above (Google, Apple, RevenueCat). You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device's settings, and change your ad consent in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS where it applies.
